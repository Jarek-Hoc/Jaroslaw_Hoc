Fixing it is not the same as learning from it

Fewer breakdown reports looks like good news. Sometimes it is. More often, people have stopped believing a report leads anywhere. Reading: Amy Edmondson, The Fearless Organization.

Monday morning
  1. Take your last ten closed analyses. Check one thing: did the reporter hear the conclusion?
  2. Once a quarter, show the team twenty analyses side by side. Twenty minutes.
  3. Before anyone books a workshop on speaking up, ask three people what happened to their last report.
